# newsletter-mvp

**Purpose:**  
Newsletter + digital product funnel MVP.

**Overview:**  
This project provides a minimal foundation for launching a newsletter with a digital product funnel. All automation is offline and safe by default.

## Structure

- **positioning/**: Niche selection and positioning.
- **emails/**: Welcome sequence and broadcast ideas.
- **products/**: Digital product concepts.
- **docs/**: Funnel workflow documentation.

## Flow

1. **Niche Selection**: Propose 3 possible newsletter niches (see below).
2. **Content Creation**: Build welcome sequence (5–7 emails), 20 broadcast ideas, 1–2 digital product concepts.
3. **Funnel Setup**: Follow workflow in docs.

## Proposed Newsletter Niches

- Personal productivity for remote workers
- Minimalist home organization
- AI tools for small business owners

## Human Input Required

- Niche selection (`positioning/`)
- Email writing and editing (`emails/`)
- Product concept refinement (`products/`)

## Instructions

1. Choose a niche in `positioning/`.
2. Draft and edit emails in `emails/`.
3. Develop product concepts in `products/`.
4. Follow funnel setup steps in `docs/`.

**Emails must be natural and readable, not marketing jargon.**
