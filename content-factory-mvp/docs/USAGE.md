# Content Factory Usage Instructions

## 1. Define Your Niche
- Edit `niche/niche.txt` with your chosen topic (e.g., "Quick healthy recipes for busy people").

## 2. Generate Content
- Video scripts are in `scripts/video_scripts.csv` (Title, Script).
- Captions are in `captions/captions.csv` (Caption).

## 3. Export Format Example
- All content is provided in CSV for easy import into social media schedulers or editing tools.
- Example (video_scripts.csv):
  | Title | Script |
  |-------|--------|
  | "5-Minute Breakfast Bowl" | "Start your day with a protein-packed breakfast bowl: microwave oats, add Greek yogurt, berries, and a sprinkle of nuts. Ready in 5 minutes!" |

- Example (captions.csv):
  | Caption |
  |---------|
  | "Breakfast in 5 minutes? Yes please! #HealthyEating #BusyLife" |

## 4. Batch Recording
- Use the scripts and captions for batch video creation.
- Record all videos in one session for efficiency.
- Captions can be copy-pasted for social posts.

## 5. Customization
- Edit scripts and captions as needed for your style or platform.
- Change the niche by updating `niche/niche.txt` and regenerating content.

## 6. Next Steps
- Use CSV files for scheduling, editing, or sharing.
- All automation is offline and safe by default.
